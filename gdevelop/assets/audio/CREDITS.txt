Project : 2050 — audio credits
All files in this folder were trimmed, mixed and re-encoded for the game.

Music (CC0 1.0)
- music-menu.mp3, music-game1.mp3, music-game2.mp3
  "Theme_1", "Ambient_1", "Ambient_2" by Maximiliano Stradex
  via https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds

Interface sounds and jingles (CC0 1.0)
- sfx-*.mp3 from Kenney "Interface Sounds", "Music Jingles", "RPG Audio" (https://kenney.nl)
  via https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds
- sfx-spawnRed.mp3 from "30 CC0 SFX loops" (alarm_01), same collection

Event ambiences (amb-*.mp3)
- Nature, animal, rain, place, transport and urban recordings from the Moodist project
  (https://github.com/remvze/moodist, commit 11c0be2). Moodist states these sounds are under
  the Pixabay Content License or CC0 (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/).
- amb-factory.mp3 mixes "machine_01" (30 CC0 SFX loops) and "sfx100v2_loop_machine_02" (100 CC0 SFX 2), CC0
- amb-explosion.mp3 mixes "explosion" (100 CC0 SFX, CC0) with a Moodist siren recording

These sounds are used inside the game only. Do not redistribute them as a standalone sound pack.
